# 13: Reflect edited, superseded, or unavailable source evidence

**What to build:** When Slack or Linear changes, a new investigation shows current applicability and availability while retaining versioned history and explaining incomplete coverage.

**Blocked by:** 02: Connect Linear history to Slack decision evidence.

**Status:** Draft — awaiting breakdown approval and project tracker setup.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Process at least one Slack edit and one Linear revision through the chosen update mechanism with resumable progress.
- [ ] Apply a scoped approved replacement without promoting a newer unapproved proposal to current policy.
- [ ] Represent a deletion/access-loss case and prevent old content being presented as newly verified current evidence.
- [ ] Retain revision lineage, supporting claims, and ontology mapping version across re-ingestion without duplicate entities.
- [ ] Expose ambiguous identity and partial import state to the requester.
- [ ] Boundary checks compare results before and after changes and after interrupted or repeated update ingestion.
